## Relevance and remaining gap

| Work | Relevance to ATIDEP | Limitation relative to ATIDEP's problem |
|---|---|---|
| SIGMERGE [@cai2026sigmerge] | Closest generator: report text to Sigma rules through an intermediate semantic layer, with a broad evaluation (23 metrics, 16 baselines, 13 LLMs). | Governance, approval, adversarial robustness, analyst effort and cost are not reported in the extracts; no deterministic gate is described. |
| AutoSigma [@ghaffarzadegan2026autosigma] | Generation from CTI with knowledge-base enrichment, retrieval of existing Sigma rules as templates and iterative validation; reports validity, relevancy and ATT&CK coverage. | Validation is by an LLM-as-a-Judge rather than deterministic gates; operational deployment is stated as future work; approval, adversarial testing, effort and cost are not reported. |
| LLMCloudHunter [@schwartz2024llmcloudhunter] | Generates Sigma rule candidates from text and images of cloud CTI and measures extraction precision and recall and compilation to Splunk. | Compilation is the only validity measure reported; governance, approval, deployment, adversarial testing, effort and cost are not reported. |
| CTI-REALM [@chakraborty2026ctirealm] | Benchmark of agents that read CTI reports, explore schemas, run queries and write detections against emulated attacks with ground truth; evaluates 16 models with trajectory-based rewards. | A benchmark of the generation task, not a governed workflow: approval, deployment, adversarial robustness of the pipeline, effort and cost are not reported. |
| Detection-as-code from attack simulation [@maiorano2026probe] | Deterministic, traceable synthesis of starter Sigma rules with replay against a live SIEM and a benign baseline; shows the value of determinism and traceability. | Starts from breach-and-attack-simulation findings, not threat reports; no LLM generation, approval, adversarial testing, effort or cost reported. |
| Static quality assessment of Sigma rules [@tyagi2026sigmalint] | Separates a binary validity gate from a weighted quality score for Sigma rules, evaluated on the SigmaHQ corpus; close to ATIDEP's gates-before-score principle. | Assesses existing rules; does not generate rules from CTI or govern their approval and deployment. |
| The Exclusion Ratchet [@dhananjeyan2026exclusion] | Empirical evidence from nine years of SigmaHQ that false-positive exclusions accumulate and persist; relevant to the feedback loop and to auditing changes. | An empirical study of a rule repository, not a generation or deployment system. |
| Tyrian Detection Pack [@zshguy2026tyrian] | Open-source Sigma-to-Wazuh compiler that refuses rules that can never fire and rewrites disjunctions into sibling rules; the closest prior art for ATIDEP's converter. | A compiler and rule pack, not a CTI-to-detection workflow: no extraction, generation from reports, approval or evaluation of effort and cost. |
| sigma_to_wazuh [@theflakes2024sigmatowazuh] | Community Sigma-to-Wazuh converter; documents why OR logic and near/timeframe constructs are hard to translate. | A converter only; conversion of some OR logic is still broken by its own account and the Python version is no longer maintained. |
| TTPDrill [@husari2017ttpdrill] | Early extraction of threat actions from unstructured CTI mapped to techniques and tactics, exportable to STIX. | Extraction only; no detection content, validation or deployment. |
| rcATT [@legoy2020rcatt] | Classification of reports into ATT&CK tactics and techniques; released tool. | Extraction only; no detection content. |
| LADDER [@alam2023ladder] | Extraction of attack patterns beyond IoCs, mapped to ATT&CK; supports the case for behaviour-based detection. | Extraction only; no detection content. |
| EXTRACTOR [@satvat2021extractor] | Extraction of attack behaviour from reports as provenance graphs usable for threat hunting. | Extraction for hunting; no rule generation, validation or deployment. |
| aCTIon [@siracusano2023action] | LLM-based (GPT-3.5) structured extraction of CTI to STIX with a 204-report dataset. | Extraction only; no detection content. |
| CTIBench [@alam2024ctibench] | Benchmark of LLMs on CTI tasks, motivated by reliability and hallucination concerns. | Evaluates LLM knowledge and task performance; does not produce or govern detection content. |
| RulePilot [@wang2025rulepilot] | LLM agent for security-rule creation and conversion through an intermediate representation; evaluated on textual similarity and execution success. | Starts from analysts' natural-language descriptions rather than threat reports; approval, deployment, adversarial testing and cost are not reported. |
| FALCON [@mitra2025falcon] | Agentic generation of deployable IDS rules (Snort, YARA) from CTI with built-in multi-phase validators and analyst assessment. | Targets network and file signatures rather than SIEM rules; human approval, deployment and rollback, effort and cost are not reported in the extracts. |
| RuleGenie [@shukla2025rulegenie] | LLM-aided optimisation of existing SIEM rule sets (redundancy and overlap), with a local open-source model compared with a proprietary one. | Optimises existing rule sets; does not generate rules from CTI; cost is only remarked on, not analysed. |
| CTI analysis agent for SOCs [@tseng2024ctiagent] | LLM agent that extracts IoCs from CTI reports and generates regular expressions for SIEM use, with consensus and retrieval checks against hallucination. | Indicator-level only; approval, deployment, adversarial testing, effort and cost are not reported in the extracts. |
| Context-aware SIEM rule generation [@schafhauzer2026context] | LLM-written local Wazuh rules from an organisation profile and the native rule catalogue, evaluated on production events: a negative result for an open-source SIEM target. | Not CTI-driven; the LLM-augmented rules were worse than the native baseline; no approval or deployment workflow reported. |
| GenTI [@hadi2026genti] | Benchmark and fine-tuned generator of IDPS and YARA rules for unseen attacks, reporting detection and false-positive rates. | Network and file signatures; approval, deployment and cost not reported; figures seen only through secondary listings. |
| TTPrint [@cheng2026ttprint] | Evidence-grounded ATT&CK extraction: deterministic span localisation ties each candidate technique to source text, close to ATIDEP's quotation verification. | Extraction only; no detection content. |
| TRACE-CTI [@valletta2026tracecti] | Governance of extracted TTP claims with evidence, provenance, policy-compliant validation, versioned trust decisions and revocation; close to ATIDEP's evidence and audit design. | Governs extraction output, not detection rules; no generation or deployment. |
| End-to-end LLM SOC framework [@saju2026autonomoussoc] | End-to-end LLM framework for alert detection, syntax-constrained SIEM query generation and resolution, reporting time savings. | Addresses alert triage and query generation rather than CTI-to-detection engineering; governance and cost are not reported. |
| SigmaGen (practitioner talk) [@pham2025sigmagen] | Practitioner pipeline extracting attack patterns from security blogs and generating Sigma with fine-tuned LLMs. | A workshop presentation: no paper, repository or benchmark found; evidence is weak. |

## Evidence for each marked cell

**SIGMERGE** [@cai2026sigmerge] (A; abstract-level search extracts; full-text verification pending)

- *CTI ingestion*: Yes. Generates Sigma rules from the texts of cyber threat reports. (source: https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity26/presentation/cai)
- *Structured extraction*: Yes. An information-extraction module extracts MITRE ATT&CK TTPs and commands. (source: https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity26/presentation/cai)
- *Behavioural detection*: Yes. Premise that shell commands described in reports can be converted into Sigma detection rules; attack-description generation module. (source: https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity26/presentation/cai)
- *Sigma / rule generation*: Yes. Sigma rule generation module. (source: https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity26/presentation/cai)
- *Deployment / rollback*: Partial. Ten case studies integrated with real security systems; no deployment or rollback procedure described in the extract. (source: https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity26/presentation/cai)

**AutoSigma** [@ghaffarzadegan2026autosigma] (A; abstract-level search extracts; full-text verification pending)

- *CTI ingestion*: Yes. Turns unstructured CTI reports (real-world APT reports and security blogs) into Sigma rules. (source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.19011)
- *Structured extraction*: Partial. Leverages a structured knowledge base to enrich partial inputs. (source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.19011)
- *Behavioural detection*: Partial. Claims gains in MITRE ATT&CK technique coverage. (source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.19011)
- *Sigma / rule generation*: Yes. Automated Sigma rule generation grounded in a repository of existing rules. (source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.19011)
- *Deployment / rollback*: No. Future work will involve validation in operational environments involving live alert streams. (source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.19011)

**LLMCloudHunter** [@schwartz2024llmcloudhunter] (A; abstract-level search extracts; full-text verification pending)

- *CTI ingestion*: Yes. Uses text and images in open-source cloud CTI. (source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05194v1)
- *Structured extraction*: Yes. Extracts API calls made by the threat actor (92% precision, 98% recall). (source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05194v1)
- *IOC generation*: Yes. Extracts IoCs with 99% precision and 98% recall for detection-rule candidates. (source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05194v1)
- *Behavioural detection*: Yes. Rule candidates built from the API calls made by the threat actor. (source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05194v1)
- *Sigma / rule generation*: Yes. Produces generic-signature (Sigma) detection-rule candidates. (source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05194v1)
- *Deterministic validation*: Partial. 99.18% of the candidates compiled and converted into Splunk queries (a compilation check, reported as an evaluation result). (source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.05194v1)

**CTI-REALM** [@chakraborty2026ctirealm] (A; abstract-level search extracts; full-text verification pending)

- *CTI ingestion*: Yes. Agents examine CTI reports. (source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13517v2)
- *Behavioural detection*: Partial. Emulated attacks on Linux, cloud and AKS, from atomic attacks to multi-step intrusions. (source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13517v2)
- *Sigma / rule generation*: Yes. Agents build detection rules; scoring covers the final detection output. (source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13517v2)
- *Deterministic validation*: Partial. Agents run queries against telemetry and outputs are scored against ground truth (part of the benchmark environment). (source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13517v2)

**Detection-as-code from attack simulation** [@maiorano2026probe] (A; abstract-level search extracts; full-text verification pending)

- *CTI ingestion*: No. Input is breach-and-attack-simulation findings mapped by a deterministic function, not threat reports. (source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.05252)
- *Behavioural detection*: Partial. Template library keyed to OWASP LLM and Web Top 10 categories; each rule linked to an ATT&CK technique. (source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.05252)
- *Sigma / rule generation*: Yes. Each finding yields a starter Sigma rule from 23 templates. (source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.05252)
- *Deterministic validation*: Partial. 17/17 emitted rules parse and convert to Splunk and Elasticsearch; replay against a live OpenSearch SIEM with false positives measured on a benign baseline (evaluation). (source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.05252)
- *Deployment / rollback*: Partial. Described as a path to an operator-deployable starter rule; replayed on a live SIEM. (source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.05252)

**Static quality assessment of Sigma rules** [@tyagi2026sigmalint] (A; abstract-level search extracts; full-text verification pending)

- *Deterministic validation*: Yes. Static quality framework that separates a binary validity gate from a weighted score, evaluated on the SigmaHQ corpus. (source: https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=6823718)

**The Exclusion Ratchet** [@dhananjeyan2026exclusion] (A; abstract-level search extracts; full-text verification pending)

- *Feedback / improvement*: Partial. Studies how false-positive suppression (exclusions) is added to and persists in rules over time; deterministic semantic suppression test. (source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.31062)

**Tyrian Detection Pack** [@zshguy2026tyrian] (R; README read directly from the repository on 2026-10-21 (software, not a paper))
