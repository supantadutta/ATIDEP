Slate Otter: reverse shell over a high port

Slate Otter is a fictional actor. After the initial script ran, the implant, a PowerShell process, connected to the staging server over TCP port 4444 to receive commands. This corresponds to MITRE ATT&CK T1071 and T1571. Defenders can alert on powershell.exe making an outbound connection to port 4444. Legitimate PowerShell traffic normally uses port 443 or 5986.
